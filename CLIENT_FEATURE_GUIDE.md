# BDIC School Management Platform

## Product overview

The School Management Platform is one digital system for the school website, admissions, daily administration, teaching, learning, payments, and parent communication. It gives the school one reliable place to manage students, teachers, classes, fees, attendance, examinations, notices, and learning activities, while giving parents and students mobile access to the information relevant to them.

## Who will use the system

| User | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| School Management / Principal | View school performance, approve key actions, monitor fees, attendance, results, staff, and notices. |
| Office / Accounts Staff | Manage admissions, student records, documents, certificates, fees, receipts, and daily office work. |
| Teachers | Take attendance, share homework, upload study material, enter marks, view timetables, and communicate with parents. |
| Students | View timetable, attendance, homework, learning materials, exam results, notices, and fee information. |
| Parents / Guardians | Track each child’s attendance, homework, exam performance, fees, notices, and school communication. |
| Specialist Staff | Librarians, transport staff, counsellors, and store staff access only the tools needed for their work. |
| Public Visitors | View school information, achievements, events, gallery, admissions information, and contact details. |

## 1. Public school website

The official school website will include the school introduction, history, vision, mission, leadership message, facilities, academic offerings, admission information, achievements, notices, events, gallery, contact details, map, office timings, social links, and a mobile-friendly enquiry form. Content can be provided in Hindi and English. The gallery and social-media-ready updates will be published only after school approval.

## 2. Online admission and onboarding

Parents can submit an enquiry, complete an application, upload documents, choose the applicable class/stream, and follow the status of the application. School staff can review eligibility, identify missing documents, schedule interviews or tests, maintain selected/waitlisted/rejected lists, collect the admission fee, and turn an accepted applicant into an active student with an admission number and portal invitation.

### Admission journey

```text
Parent submits enquiry
→ Parent completes application
→ School verifies documents and eligibility
→ School schedules test/interview where required
→ School selects, waitlists, or rejects the application
→ Parent pays admission fee
→ Student receives admission number and class allocation
→ Parent and student receive portal access
```

## 3. Student and staff records

Each student will have one permanent digital profile containing photograph, admission information, academic history, guardian details, contact details, address, emergency contacts, documents, approved medical/support notes, and certificates. The system will preserve history when a student changes class, is promoted, transferred, withdrawn, or becomes an alumnus.

Each staff profile will keep role, department, qualification, joining details, documents, assigned classes/subjects, timetable, attendance, leave, and internal notices.

## 4. Academic planning and timetables

The school can set academic years, terms, classes, sections, streams, subjects, elective subjects, grading scales, holidays, houses, and activities. Teachers, rooms, classes, and subjects are assigned to an interactive timetable. Students, parents, and teachers see their own daily schedule. Substitute duties and timetable changes can be communicated immediately.

## 5. Attendance

Teachers mark daily attendance by class and section. The system supports present, absent, late, leave, and medical leave statuses, with a controlled correction process. Parents receive absence alerts when enabled. School management can monitor class, student, and school-wide attendance, including low-attendance warnings.

```text
Teacher opens today’s assigned class
→ Marks student attendance
→ System records absences and late entries
→ Parent receives alert where enabled
→ Class teacher/principal reviews low-attendance cases
→ Attendance becomes part of the student academic record
```

## 6. Homework and digital learning

Teachers can publish homework, instructions, worksheets, notes, photos, PDFs, videos, and learning links by class and subject. Students and parents see deadlines and pending work. Where enabled, students submit work online and teachers provide feedback. Later releases can add courses, quizzes, question banks, recorded/live class links, and learning-progress reports.

## 7. Examinations, results, and report cards

The school can prepare exam schedules, configure assessment rules, collect subject marks, handle absent/exempt/re-exam cases, review marks, and approve results before publication. Students and parents can view results and download report cards. Management can view class and subject performance. Promotion, supplementary examination, and transfer rules will follow approved school policy.

```text
School creates examination plan
→ Teachers enter subject marks
→ Authorized staff reviews entries
→ Principal/management approves results
→ Report cards are generated
→ Results are published to students and parents
→ Promotion and next-class planning begins
```

## 8. Fees and payments

The fees area manages class-wise fee structures, installments, admission fees, tuition, transport, examination and other fee heads. It supports due dates, late fees, concessions, scholarships, sibling discounts, waivers, refunds, receipts, and outstanding dues. Parents can pay through the selected online payment provider or at the school office using cash/cheque procedures approved by the school. Accounts staff see daily collection, due, concession, and reconciliation reports.

```text
School defines fee plan
→ Parent sees dues in portal
→ Parent pays online or at the school office
→ Payment is confirmed
→ Receipt is issued
→ Accounts team sees collection and pending-due reports
→ Reminders are sent for unpaid installments
```

## 9. Role-based portals

### Parent portal

Parents can see each linked child’s timetable, attendance, homework, study material, assignments, results, report cards, fees, receipts, notices, events, documents, consent requests, and approved teacher communication.

### Student portal

Students can see their timetable, attendance, homework, learning material, assignment deadlines, submissions, exam schedule, results, notices, events, and permitted documents.

### Teacher portal

Teachers can see today’s classes and timetable, take attendance, access their assigned student/class information, post homework, review submissions, enter marks, review progress trends, communicate using approved channels, and request corrections through an approval process.

### School administration portal

Leadership and authorized staff can manage admissions, school structure, student and staff records, fees, reports, approvals, notices, certificates, and operational dashboards.

## 10. Notices and communication

The school can send notices to the entire school or selected classes, sections, staff, parents, or students. Delivery can use portal notifications and selected SMS, WhatsApp, email, or push services. Important notices can require acknowledgement. Parent-teacher messaging will have school-defined timing, access, and moderation rules. Communication history is retained for accountability.

## 11. Events, achievements, gallery, and community

The platform will support event calendars, registrations, participation lists, consent forms, school achievements, toppers, certificates, moderated galleries, and future alumni registration. It provides a controlled workflow for public and social-media content so student images and stories are published only with the correct approval and consent.

## End-to-end student journey

```text
Public website visit
→ Admission enquiry
→ Application and document submission
→ School review and selection
→ Fee payment and enrollment
→ Student/parent account activation
→ Class, subject, teacher, and timetable allocation
→ Daily attendance and homework
→ Parent communication and fee reminders
→ Exams, results, report cards, and promotion
→ Certificates, transfer, or alumni record when the student leaves
```

- [ ] Approved photographs, videos, achievements, and gallery content
- [ ] Official social-media links

- [ ] Board requirements and academic calendar
- [ ] Attendance, leave, grading, rank, promotion, and supplementary-exam rules
- [ ] Mark-correction and result-approval process

- [ ] Cash/cheque policy and preferred payment provider

- [ ] Required channels: SMS, WhatsApp, email, and/or app notification

- [ ] Super Admins, office staff, accounts staff, teacher, and specialist-staff roles
- [ ] Parent/student login and recovery approach
